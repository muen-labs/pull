PULL — submission video narration
Voiced in ElevenLabs, then dropped into DaVinci Resolve with the screen recording.
Paste this file as-is (blank lines are pauses). The timed version is pull-submission-video.srt.

── Act 1 · the board ─────────────────────────────────────────────

Every season, a stylist gets hundreds of runway screenshots.

This is PULL — the whole job, from screenshots to signed-off looks.

Drop in the folder.

The agent reads every caption and trims it.

Every look is filed under its house, ready to shortlist.

Nothing is published until you say so.

── Act 2 · the client link ───────────────────────────────────────

Send one link. Your client opens it with no account, no app.

They approve what they love, and pass the rest.

And they can circle the exact piece they want, right on the look.

That markup comes back to your board.

── Act 3 · the requests ──────────────────────────────────────────

From the approvals, the agent drafts one email per showroom.

Every approved look, the shoot dates, the ship-to address.

You read it, edit it, and approve it.

Nothing leaves the studio until you approve it.

── Act 4 · the studio, on ZooWork ────────────────────────────────

The studio is where ZooWork does the heavy lifting.

Four references, and a border you drew around one of them.

The ZooWork agent reads the pictures and writes the edit prompt.

GPT Image 2 renders the look — ZooWork's image model.

Vision and image, both from ZooWork.

Every call is mirrored to PRISM, with the model and the latency.

── Act 5 · suggest looks ─────────────────────────────────────────

The suggestions start from the client's own record.

What they wore, and for which house.

Then eight looks from each house's runway, each collection linked to its source.

── Act 6 · close ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every tool call and every model call, in the open.

Models by ZooWork.

Traces by PRISM.

PULL. From screenshots to signed-off looks.
